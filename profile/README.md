## About LiquidText

LiquidText is a focused document review environment for people who need to read deeply, extract meaning, and keep sources connected. Instead of treating a PDF as a flat stack of pages, LiquidText document review lets you pull passages, create visual links, and build a personal research workspace that stays readable over long sessions. LiquidText PDF annotation supports students, researchers, legal readers, and analysts who want their notes to remain attached to the exact source text. The workspace is designed for meaning extraction, not just highlighting. You can gather fragments from multiple documents, arrange them spatially, and compare research projects side by side. LiquidText note extraction keeps important passages visible while you continue reading, so a thought is never lost between pages. For anyone who works with long reports, academic papers, contracts, or case files, LiquidText research workspace offers a steady path from first scan to final synthesis. LiquidText project comparison helps you revisit earlier reading, check how sources relate, and keep a clear record of what changed. The goal is simple: make document review feel less like scrolling and more like thinking.
